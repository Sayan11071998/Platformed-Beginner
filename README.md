# 🚀 Fire Platform 🔥🎮

I’m excited to finally share the gameplay of my latest project, **Fire Platform**!  
Picture this: navigating through blazing fire obstacles, collecting shiny objects, and solving puzzles—all while trying not to get burned (literally).  
This game puts your reflexes to the test while also challenging your brain with a puzzle or two along the way.

---

## What I Learned

- **Game Design & Mechanics**  
  Mastered player movement and rotation to effectively dodge fire and survive challenging scenarios.

- **Advanced C++ Programming**  
  Built complex gameplay mechanics, significantly sharpening my coding skills.

- **Game Level Design**  
  Designed immersive levels filled with dynamic fire hazards and collectible items.

- **Fire Damage & Health System**  
  Implemented a damage-over-time system where players must avoid fire to preserve health and survive longer.

- **Puzzle Logic**  
  Integrated collectible-driven puzzle elements that trigger doors or path changes to deepen gameplay.

- **Troubleshooting & Debugging**  
  Resolved performance and logic issues to ensure a smooth and stable experience.

---

## Final Thoughts

This project was a fantastic opportunity to dive deeper into Unreal Engine’s Blueprint system while expanding my knowledge of C++ integration.  
I’m proud of the result and excited to keep pushing my skills further in future projects!
